 The goal of the **WorldVegChecklist** initiative is to inventory and revise all described syntaxa at the class level globally. A standardized entry form has been developed, covering key categories: such as continent, country, region, broad vegetation type, biogeographic region, subbiome, altitudinal belt, distribution, description (ecology, physiognomy), validity, protologue, type, diagnostic species in the original diagnosis.
In addition to cataloguing vegetation classes, an important aspect of this initiative is aligning each class with corresponding units from the EcoVeg approach to vegetation classification. Each class will be matched to EcoVeg units, such as formation class, formation subclass, formation, and division. This ensures a comprehensive integration of global vegetation classification frameworks.

The structure of the table is still being refined and will be further developed in the coming year. The group aims to present this initiative at the IAVS conference in Colorado in 2025 and promote it through the IAVS Bulletin.
Further details will be provided in the nearest future!
